Bagel Breakfast Boats

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Cook Time: 20 minutes

Total time: 30 minutes

These bagel breakfast boats are my go-to when I want something cozy but a little more fun than the usual eggs and toast. The bagels get lightly crisp on the outside while soaking up that cheesy egg filling inside, almost like a built-in breakfast sandwich situation. You can switch up the fillings however you want, so it never gets old.
